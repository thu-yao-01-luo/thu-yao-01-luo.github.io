- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27370)

This report presents an open, cost-efficient recipe for pretraining 2B language models on consumer-grade RTX 5090 GPUs. It releases the training data, code, and model weights, and studies how hardware, low-precision training, optimization, curriculum model averaging, and data choices affect cost and performance.

I led the project and am a co-first author, listed first. The recipe demonstrates trillion-token FP8 pretraining on consumer GPUs.
